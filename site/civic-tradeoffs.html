METHODOLOGY / SCOPE

Show the workings.

Every number should travel with a plain-language explanation of what it represents and what it leaves out.

What exactly is calculated?

Each selected cost reduction and revenue option is counted once. The optional general-revenue lever adds an integer amount in $100,000 increments. Covered gap = selected cost reductions + selected revenue options + general-revenue lever. Remaining = max(0, scenario gap - covered); surplus = max(0, covered - scenario gap). All calculations use exact integer dollars.

Where do the figures come from?

They were authored as a synthetic educational fixture for Commonwealth, not extracted from DeLand, an actual city budget, fiscal projections, voter preferences, or property records. The baseline starts with $12.4M in service costs and $10.0M in funding. The stress case holds services fixed and reduces funding by $800,000.

Is a balanced plan automatically a good policy?

No. A numerical match is only one constraint. Real governments must evaluate statutory duties, equity, eligibility, staffing, timing, unintended consequences, alternatives, source quality, public consultation and representative authority. Commonwealth's Warrant treats evidence and legal authority as separate questions.

Are my choices collected or made public?

No. The page makes no preference API requests, uses no local or session storage, and does not send your selections to the Commonwealth project or a government office. Copy and download actions run only after you press the relevant button. Do not mistake a downloaded receipt for an official submission.
